What We Do

From the floor beneath your feet to the ceiling overhead — the details that make a room feel finished.

Veneer Plaster & Paint

Smooth, rich wall and ceiling finishes that ordinary paint can't match. From durable veneer plaster to clean modern color, every surface is prepped and finished by hand.

  • Veneer plaster systems & finishes
  • Interior painting — walls, ceilings & trim
  • Plaster and drywall repair
  • Ceiling texture & refinishing

Flooring — LVP, LVT & Hardwood

Quality flooring installed right the first time — level, tight, and built to last. From waterproof vinyl to classic hardwood, we help you pick the floor that fits your home and your budget.

  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) installation
  • Luxury vinyl tile (LVT) installation
  • Hardwood flooring installation
  • Subfloor prep, transitions & trim-out

Finish Carpentry

The trim work that ties a room together. Precise cuts, tight joints, and clean lines — installed with the care your home deserves.

  • Crown molding & trim installation
  • Baseboards, casing & wainscoting
  • Interior doors & hardware
  • Custom built-ins & detail work

About
